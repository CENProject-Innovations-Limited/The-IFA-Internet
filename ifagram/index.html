What is IfaDiagram?

IfaDiagram (Ifa Diagram) is the Diagram for Everything (DiagroE) — the general Tool and System for Graphical and Visual Languages of the IFA Internet. It provides standardised symbols, operators, and diagram types for visual communication across all knowledge fields of the IFA Internet. Core instrument of Ifa Calculus, Ifa Analysis, and the IFA Body of Knowledge (IFABOK).

Also known as: DiagroE (Diagram for Everything), ToE Diagram, Energy-Based Diagram, Consciousness Diagram, Ifa Visual Language System, Orisa Diagram System.

Key Ifagrams include: the Lemniscate Cross (s² = r² cos 2θ), the Ifa Fractal (self-similar lemniscate), IfaZero, IfaOne, the Ifa Sigma (Temic Sigma), and the Squaket Operator (IfaBrackets).
