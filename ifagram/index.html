The Ifa Infinity (Ọpọ́n Ifá Infinity)

Also known as Ọpọ́nfá Infinity — the Ifa Infinity expressed through the Sacred Geometry of the Ọpọ́n Ifá (Ifa Divination Board), i.e., IfaGeometry. Each of the four lobes of the Ifa Infinity is a sacred Ọpọ́n Ifá — four divination boards arranged in the cardinal directions of the Axiomatic Odù Structure, unified by the Ogbenergy.

◉
Ọpọ́n Ifá — The Sacred Divination Board
The Ọpọ́n Ifá is the sacred circular tray of Ifá divination — the instrument through which Ọrúnmìlà's wisdom is consulted. Carved with ancestral and Orisa figures around its rim, with a flat centre surface for Iyẹrẹ Ifá (divination powder), it is the original DiagroE — the Diagram for Everything in Ifa Tradition.
∞
Ọpọ́nfá Infinity — Four Boards, One Infinity
In the Ọpọ́nfá Infinity, the four lobes of the Ifa Infinity — East, West, North, South — each become an Ọpọ́n Ifá. The Ogbenergy, also known as Ogbergy (in short), connects them as the eternal flow of Àṣẹ across all 256 Odu Ifa and all realms of existence. The IfaBoards face outward — their carved ancestral Figures witnessing the Infinite Field of Potentiality (IfaPotentiality).
⊕
The Original Diagram for Everything
The Ọpọ́nfá Infinity reveals that the Ọpọ́n Ifá is itself a DiagroE. Its circular form, carved ancestral figures, and divination centre encode the full structure of Ifa knowledge — the 256 Odu Ifa, the Orisa Field, and the Totality of Existence (TOE) — unified in the Geometry of the Ifa Infinity.

IfaConstruction

The 256 Ọpọ́nfá Infinities — one for each Odù Ifá — are built through the Àmúlù Operation, also called IfaComposition.

The process begins with the Ọpọ́n Ifá itself, or equivalently the IfaZero (IfaCircle), which represents Ogbe Energy (Ogbenergy) — the Primal, Undivided Circle of Àṣẹ.

Four such Ọpọ́n Ifá structures are composed together through the Àmúlù Operation, producing the Òbìrìkìtí (Dual: Òkìtìbìrí) — the Ifa Infinity, meaning Immense Energy.

In modern mathematics, a related form appears as the Lemniscate of Bernoulli — constructed by taking the inverse (geometric inversion) of a hyperbola, or equivalently as the locus of points whose product of distances from two fixed foci equals the square of half the distance between them. Its equation is given in Cartesian form as (x² + y²)² = 2a²(x² − y²), or in polar form as r² = 2a² cos(2θ).

However, the Òbìrìkìtí and its Dual Òkìtìbìrí are far older and carry a deeper ontology: they are not merely geometric curves but Expressions of the Composition of Ogbe Energy and Oyeku Anergy (Oyekunergy/Oyekergy) across the Four Cardinal Axes of Odù Ifá System.
