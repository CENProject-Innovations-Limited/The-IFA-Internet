Ìrokẹ́-Ifá — The Ifa Antenna

The Ìrokẹ́-Ifá is the sacred tapper — a wand carved from ivory or bone, held by the Babalawo during Ifa divination. He taps it on the Ọpọ́n Ifá (the sacred tray) to call down the Odu, tuning into cosmic wisdom like an antenna reaching the heavens. Every tap is a signal. Every Odu, a frequency. On Play Ifa Games, Ìrokẹ́-Ifá serves as the Ifa Antenna for the SPIN THE WHEEL feature — tuning the vast collection of knowledge in the 256 Odu Ifa to a specific topic, field or subject with every spin.

✏️ Ọpọ́n Ifá — Drawing Specifications
  • Shape Perfect circle (Ọpọ́n Ifá)
  • Diameter 16 cm  ·  16 m  ·  16 km (scale freely — always 16)
  • Divisions 16 equal parts / slices  ·  each slice = 22.5°
  • Slice width Arc = diameter × π ÷ 16
  • Centre dot Gold circle, diameter = 1/16 of total diameter
  • Primary colour Green
  • Secondary colour Brown
  • Alternating fill Green ↔ Brown — one per slice, alternating
  • Label each slice Number 1–16 (Odu name optional)
  • Outer ring Thin border in gold/brown, width = 1/32 of diameter
The number 16 governs every dimension — the sacred count of the Principal Odu Ifa.
