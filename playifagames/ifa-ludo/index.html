How to play IfáLudo

The IfaDice. Both dice carry all sixteen principal Odù, Ifá one to Ifá sixteen, so each lands on one of 16 (Òòṣà Dídá, 1/16) and together they make one of the 256 Odù Ifá (Ifá Dídá, 1/256), right leg first, as in Ogbè-Ìwòrì. Ifa Octagon (Oníláméjọfá): a faceted solid with an octagon outline and sixteen faces: Ifá one to Ifá eight ring the front square and Ifá nine to Ifá sixteen ring the back. The die turns to show the side that landed, the face glows gold, and its Odù shows large on the front square. IfaCube: the same sixteen Odù on the corners of an Ifa Hypercube, carried on the three arms of a cube.

The board. Four pathways meet at the centre. Each pathway is four lanes of eight boxes: the two outer lanes and the end of the pathway form the shared track (72 squares, 18 per player); the two middle lanes, both in your colour, are your two home lanes, Ọ̀tún (right) and Òsì (left).

Two dice, two moves. Each die counts the single marks of its own leg, 0 to 4: the Ọ̀tún (right) die and the Òsì (left) die. Use each die on a token, so two tokens can move in one cast (or the same token twice), or add both dice together and move one token by the total, 0 to 8. Choose the die under the reading, then a glowing token.

Leaving home. A Méjì (both dice the same Odù) or a cast with 6 or more single marks in all lets you bring one token onto your starting square. Bringing it out spends one die; the other die can still move.

Coming home. After going once around, a token turns into one of your two home lanes. At the end of your own pathway you choose: the Ọ̀tún lane or the Òsì lane. Inside a home lane a token moves only by its own die: in Ọ̀tún by the Ọ̀tún die, in Òsì by the Òsì die (0 to 4), never by the sum. It needs an exact count to reach the centre.

Méjì. Any Méjì (1 cast in 16) earns another cast.

Capturing. Land exactly on an opponent token on the track to send it home and earn another cast. Starting squares and starred squares are safe; home lanes belong to their owner.

Two players. Each player plays two bases on opposite corners: Player 1 has Gold and Red, Player 2 has Green and Blue. The bases take turns in order around the board, so the two players alternate. Your two bases never capture each other.

Winning. The first player to bring all four tokens into the centre wins. With two players, you need all eight tokens home, on both of your bases.

Boards and dice. Play on the square Ọpọ́n (Ọpọ́nfá Onílámẹ́rin Ogbọ́ọ́gba), the rectangular Ọpọ́n (Ọpọ́nfá Onílámẹ́rin) or the IfaCircle (Òbìrìfá), with the Ifa Octagon (Oníláméjọfá) or the IfaCube dice; switch at any time.
