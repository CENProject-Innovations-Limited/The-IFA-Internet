Start Tinkering with Ifa & Orisa

Pick a project. It opens in the right tool, already set up, with the steps to follow. Every project works in IfaLang, and every circuit has its Dual in OrisaLang.

01 · BeginnerGates

Light the Ogbe lamp

  1. Two switches feed one Àti (AND) gate.
  2. Tap each switch until it shows a single mark (ı).
  3. The lamp lights only when both are Ogbe.
02 · BeginnerCircuits

Read the Odu

  1. Four switches feed the Odu display.
  2. Set the marks to show Iwori, then Odi.
  3. Try all 16 principal Odu.
03 · IntermediateCircuits

Add in IfaLang: the half adder

  1. Ọ̀kan péré (XOR) gives the sum.
  2. Àti (AND) gives the carry.
  3. Open the truth table to check all four cases.
04 · IntermediateDuality

Meet the Orisa Dual

  1. Load an Àti (AND) circuit.
  2. Switch the bench to OrisaLang.
  3. Every signal flips, and Àti becomes Tàbí (OR).
05 · IntermediateBlueprints

Draft an Ọpọ́nfá pavilion

  1. Four round halls sit on the lobes of the Ifa Infinity.
  2. Move a hall and watch its dimensions update.
  3. Export the blueprint as SVG.
06 · ExplorerSimulation

Count all 256

  1. Two Odu make eight marks: 2⁸ = 256 states.
  2. Press Run and watch the register count.
  3. Invert to see each state’s Orisa Dual.

Àrògúnfá · IfaLogic

The Ifa Logic System

IfaLogic is the Logic of Everything, grounded in the 16 principal Odu Ifa. Each Odu is a Law of Nature, so each Odu carries its own logic: Ogbe Logic (OgbeLogic), Oyeku Logic (OyekuLogic), and so on to Ofun Logic (OfunLogic). Each logic rules one of the 16 IfaGates.

∀
IfaLogic · ÀrògúnfáGrounded in Ogbe (ı), active energy. It speaks For All (∀): a law that holds everywhere.
∃
OrisaLogic · ÀrògúnòòṣàThe Dual of IfaLogic, grounded in Oyeku (॥), anergy. It speaks There Exists (∃).
ı॥
Two readings of the same marksIn circuits, Ilé Arọ́ reads a single mark (ı) as Ogbe energy, 1, as IfaLogic does. The Ifa Periodic Table’s IFABit writes the double mark as 1, so OgbeBit is 0000. Each reading is the other’s Dual.

The 16 Ifa Logic Systems

Each card is one Odu’s Law of Nature, read as logic. The SIDECHRX principles pair the 16 Odu into eight pairs, and each pair rules a gate and its Dual. Tap a card to open its IfaGate in the lab.

Boards in Yoruba: Ọpọ́n-Àrògún àti Ọpọ́n-Ifá

Ọpọ́n-ÀrògúnLogic BoardA trainer board that lays out logic gates with switches for inputs and lights for outputs. Àrògún is logic; ọpọ́n is a board or tray.
Ọpọ́n-IfáIfa BoardThe carved Ifa tray on which the Odu are marked. It is the first board of Ifa logic: 16 Odu, read from the same face.
Ọpọ́n-Àrògún AyíbíríPrinted Circuit Board (PCB)A logic board with its circuits printed in copper. The circuit board in the workbench below is an Ọpọ́n-Àrògún Ayíbírí.

Ọpọ́n-Ifá and the PCB are two boards for the same work: the Ifa tray holds the Odu that the logic comes from, and the PCB holds the gates built from that logic. Below, the 16 IfaGates sit on both.

The 16 IfaGates on the Ọpọ́n

The 16 IfaGates are the Builders, Connectors, Integrators and Containers of every field, technology and system. Flip a switch on the Ọpọ́n-Àrògún, or switch to the Ọpọ́n-Ifá to see all 16 gates read the same two marks. Tap any gate to open it in the lab.

Tool 3 · Models & simulations

Odu Register Simulator

Two Odu side by side make eight marks: 2⁸ = 256 states, the 256 Odu Ifa. Read right to left. Tap any mark to flip it, step through the states, or run the register on a clock and watch it count.

Ìṣèṣe Engineering · Isesengineering

Engineering in Yoruba

Ifa-Orisa Engineering builds in two dual languages. Ifaided designs are built in IfaLang, and Orisaided designs are built in OrisaLang. In Ilé Arọ́, every circuit has its Dual: flip each mark, and Àti becomes Tàbí.

Ìṣèṣe Engineering (Isesengineering) is engineering rooted in Ìṣèṣe — Yoruba heritage and its ways of knowing — and spoken in Yoruba. The words below name the parts and tools of this workshop.
