Iṣẹ́ Ọnà Ayárabíàṣá

Computer-Aided Design (CAD) in Yoruba

Iṣẹ́ Ọnà Ayárabíàṣá is design with the computer in Yoruba — the IfaLabs home of Computer-Aided Design. It works in two dual languages: designs built in Ifa Language (IfaLang) are Ifa-Aided Designs (IfaCAD), and designs built in Orisa Language (OrisaLang) are Orisa-Aided Designs (OrisaCAD). Together, Ifa-Orisa Designs serve every field of STEAM and every profession.

Àmúlù · IfaComposition

Compose the Ifa Infinity by hand

The 256 Ọpọ́nfá Infinities are built through the Àmúlù Operation, also called IfaComposition. It begins with the Ọpọ́n Ifá — the IfaZero (IfaCircle) — and four such circles composed together produce the Òbìrìkìtí, the Ifa Infinity. Draw circles, ellipses and lobes; move, resize and rotate them; and combine them into the Ifa Infinity design, or a design of your own.
