What is IfaLabs?

IfaLabs (TOELabs) is the research and innovation lab of the IFA Internet — integrating Ifa Engineering, Ifa Mechanics, Ifa Analysis, Ifa Modelling, the Ifa Periodic Table, and Ifa Games. Grounded in the E.T.H.I.C.S Framework and the 256 Odu Ifa as the axiomatic foundation.

Also known as: TOELabs, Theory of Everything Labs, IFA Labs, CENProject Innovation Hub.
