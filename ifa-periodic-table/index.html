What is the Ifa Periodic Table?

The Ifa Periodic Table (IfaPToE) is the Periodic Table of Everything — 256 Odu Ifa arranged as the Standard Model of Every Knowledge, consciousness, and energy. Each Odu (Ifatom) carries its IFABit binary code, Yoruba name, and meaning as a building block of all existence.

Also known as: IfaPToE, Periodic Table of Everything, Ifatom Table, Standard Model of Knowledge, OduCell Table.
