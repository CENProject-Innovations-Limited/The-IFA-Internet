What is IfaLang?

IfaLang is the mathematical Language of Ifa and Orisa — the universal language of the IFA Internet encoded in the 256 Odu Ifa. It comprises 16 root Odu Ifa phonemes, 256 composite words, and an energy-based grammar and composition system rooted in Yoruba symbolic tradition.

Also known as: Ifa Language, Language of Ifa, IFA Language, OgbeSymbol Language, IfaGebra Language.
