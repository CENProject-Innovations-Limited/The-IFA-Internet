In Ifa tradition, every body part, organ, and physiological system has a corresponding spiritual governance encoded within the 256 Odu. Odu marks the cells within the body and the space between cells — operating at the most fundamental biological level. Illness is diagnosed by divination, which reveals the governing Odu, which in turn prescribes the remedy (Akose Ifa).

Ejiogbe

Head (Ori), spinal column, sternum, heart (mitral valve), blood vessels, lymph nodes
02 · Spirit of Darkness

Òyèkú Méjì — Contraction
Spleen, kidneys, uterus, internal organs — signals organ failure or loss

04 · The Seal

05 · Head Protector

05  ·  Core Philosophical Principles
🔬
Cellular Governance
Odu marks the cells within the body and the spaces between them — Ifa operates at the most fundamental biological level.
⚖️
Polarity & Balance
Right side (male/expansion) vs. left side (female/contraction). Balance between Ori (thought) and heart (emotion) is foundational.
🌿
Holistic Causation
Illness has three root types: Spiritual imbalance, Ancestral disruption, and Natural / herbal deficiency. The Odu identifies which.
🧬
256 Odu = All Conditions
Every possible disease state and health outcome is encoded somewhere in the 256 Odu corpus — the DNA of creation.
🛡️
Spiritual Custodianship
Every organ has an associated Orisa. Healing requires both physical and spiritual alignment — one cannot be treated without the other.
♾️
Energy as Medicine
Disease is a disruption of energy flow. Ebo and Akose restore the energetic signature encoded in the body by the governing Odu.
