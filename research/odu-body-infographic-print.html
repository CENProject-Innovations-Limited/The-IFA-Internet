In Ifa tradition, every body part, organ, and physiological system has a corresponding spiritual governance encoded within the 256 Odu. Odu marks the cells within the body and the space between cells — operating at the most fundamental biological level. Illness is diagnosed by divination, which reveals the governing Odu, which in turn prescribes the remedy (Akose Ifa).

01 · The 16 Principal Odu Meji — Body Governance
01 · King of Odu
Ejiogbe
Èjì Ogbè — Source of Light
Governs
Head (Ori), spinal column, sternum, heart (mitral valve), blood vessels, lymph nodes
02 · Spirit of Darkness
Oyeku Meji
Òyèkú Méjì — Contraction
Governs
Spleen, kidneys, uterus, internal organs — signals organ failure or loss
03 · The Deep Seer
Iwori Meji
Ìwòrí Méjì — Inner Sight
Governs
Inner neurological awareness, consciousness, cognitive essence
04 · The Seal
Odi Meji
Òdí Méjì — Birth & Fertility
Governs
Female reproductive organs, uterus, cardiovascular system, tonsils, throat
05 · Head Protector
Irosun Meji

Governs
Head (Ori), memory centers, blood — ancestral lineage connection

Governs
Hidden/latent disease, old age degeneration, sudden collapse
07 · The Resting One

Personal will, self-healing capacity, limbs and joints
12 · The Bearer
Oturupon Meji

Sudden death risk, chronic sickness, latent tribulations requiring urgent Ebo

03 · Orisa — Spiritual Body Custodians
Orunmila
Head (Ori) — master of divination and healing; source of all Ifa medical prescriptions
Osun (Oshun)
Head, memory, blood in veins — protects the Ori and ancestral memory (Irosun Meji)
Sango (Shango)
Heart — "pushes death away from the heart"; governs cardiac vital force and strength
Yemaya
Womb, amniotic fluid, breasts, water in the body — governs all fluid systems (Odi Meji)
Obatala
Head mold (skull), facial orifices — eyes, ears, nose, mouth; the white matter of the body
Oshumare
Abdomen, arms, hands, feet movement — motor nervous system and spinal cord
Osanyin
Plant & herbal medicine — every organ's botanical remedy encoded in Ifa verses
Elegua (Eshu)
Feet, pathways within the body, nervous system connections and destiny

05 · Core Philosophical Principles of Ifa Medicine
🔬
Cellular Governance
Odu marks the cells within the body and the spaces between them. Ifa operates at the most fundamental biological level, encoding cellular biology in spiritual language — the DNA of creation.
⚖️
Polarity & Balance
Right side (male/expansion) vs. left side (female/contraction). Balance between Ori (thought/head) and heart (emotion) is the foundational law of physical health and wellness.
🌿
Holistic Causation
Illness has three root types: Spiritual imbalance, Ancestral disruption, and Natural/herbal deficiency. The Odu identifies which root applies — and prescribes the exact corresponding remedy.
🧬
256 Odu = All Conditions
Every possible disease state, bodily condition, and health outcome is encoded somewhere in the 256 Odu corpus. The Odu are the complete medical encyclopedia of the universe.
🛡️
Spiritual Custodianship
Every organ has an associated Orisa and spiritual responsibility. Healing requires both physical and spiritual alignment simultaneously — one cannot be treated without the other.
♾️
Energy as Medicine
Disease is a disruption of energy flow. Ebo (offering) and Akose (herbal-spiritual medicine) restore the energetic signature encoded in the body by the governing Odu.

Research Note

The full systematic mapping of all 256 Odu to specific body parts exists within oral traditions and the Ifa literary corpus. The associations above represent what has been documented in accessible scholarly and practitioner sources. For complete medical knowledge, consultation with an initiated Babalawo or Iyánífá is essential. Sources include UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage registry, IFA College (ifacollege.ng), Ileifa (ileifa.org), and peer-reviewed African traditional medicine research.
