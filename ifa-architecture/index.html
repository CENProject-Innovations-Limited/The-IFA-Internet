What is Ifa Architecture?

Ifa Architecture (Ifarch) is the Architecture for Everything (ArchoE) platform of the IFA Internet — studying all fields of architecture through the Energy-based Principles of Ifa. The 256 Odu Ifa serve as the Blueprint of the IFA Internet and all existence.

Also known as: Ifarch, ArchoE, ToE Architecture, Energy-Based Architecture, Orisa Architecture, Orisarch.
